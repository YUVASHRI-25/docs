## Get Started in 4 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up with your email or use Google OAuth. Your role (job seeker, trainer, or admin) determines which features and dashboards you can access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Your Resume">
    Drag and drop a PDF or DOCX file. Resume Insight extracts your education, skills, certifications, and experience automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Your Analysis">
    Receive an ATS compatibility score, skill gap suggestions, and a predicted job role — all generated in seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act on Guidance">
    Use the AI Chat assistant or career roadmap to understand exactly which skills to add and how to improve your resume.
  </Step>
</Steps>
