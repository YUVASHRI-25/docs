As a job seeker on Resume Insight, you get a complete career toolkit the moment you upload your resume. The platform automatically extracts your profile, scores your resume against ATS standards, predicts the best-fit job role for your skill set, highlights the skills you should learn next, and gives you access to an AI chat assistant for personalized career advice — all without any manual configuration on your end.

## Your Dashboard

Follow these steps each time you log in and want to analyze your resume:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload your resume">
    From your dashboard, click **Upload Resume** and select a PDF or DOCX file. Resume Insight supports resumes up to standard sizes and will reject unsupported formats.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your extracted profile">
    Once processed, your dashboard displays the structured data extracted from your resume: your name, contact details, education history (10th, 12th, and bachelor's degree with CGPA), technical and soft skills, certifications, projects, and known programming languages.

    Review this section carefully — the accuracy of all downstream analysis depends on what was successfully parsed from your file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your ATS score and breakdown">
    Your ATS score appears as a percentage, accompanied by a detailed breakdown. This score reflects how likely your resume is to pass automated filtering systems used by most companies. Higher scores improve your chances of reaching a human reviewer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See your predicted role and suggested skills">
    The AI determines the job role that best matches your profile — for example, **Data Analyst**, **Frontend Developer**, or **Machine Learning Engineer** — and lists 5–8 skills you should add to your repertoire to become a stronger candidate for that role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate career guidance or chat with the AI">
    Click **Generate Guidance** to receive a structured, AI-generated roadmap for your career. Or open the **AI Chat** tab to ask specific questions such as: *"How can I transition from web development to cloud engineering?"* The assistant uses your resume as context for every answer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Resume Upload

To upload your resume and receive a full analysis, send a `multipart/form-data` POST request to `/user/upload_resume` with your email address and resume file.

```bash
curl -X POST http://localhost:8000/user/upload_resume \
  -F "email=alex@example.com" \
  -F "file=@/path/to/resume.pdf"
```

**Form fields:**

<ParamField body="email" type="string" required>
  Your registered email address. Used to store and retrieve your resume analysis results.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="file" type="file" required>
  Your resume file. Accepted formats: `.pdf`, `.docx`. The file is processed immediately — no separate parse step is required.
</ParamField>

**Example response:**

<ResponseField name="status" type="string">

<ResponseField name="suggested_skills" type="array">

</ResponseField>

## Career Guidance

After uploading your resume, you can generate a detailed career roadmap using the `POST /guidance/generate` endpoint. Pass your `structured_info` object (returned from the upload step) as the `resume_data` field in the request body.

```bash
curl -X POST http://localhost:8000/guidance/generate \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "resume_data": {
      "name": "Alex Johnson",
      "skills": {
        "technical": ["Python", "React", "MongoDB"],
        "area_of_interest": ["Full Stack Development"]
      },
      "education": {
        "bachelor": { "degree": "B.E. Computer Science", "cgpa": "8.45" }
      }
    }
  }'
```

**Example response structure:**

```json
{
  "guidance": {
    "current_profile_summary": "You have a strong foundation in full-stack development with skills in Python, React, and MongoDB.",
    "recommended_role": "Full Stack Developer",
    "short_term_goals": [
      "Complete a TypeScript course and refactor one of your existing React projects",
      "Deploy a personal project on AWS using EC2 or Elastic Beanstalk",
      "Contribute to an open-source project on GitHub"
    ],
    "long_term_goals": [
      "Target mid-level full-stack roles at product companies within 12–18 months",
      "Build expertise in cloud-native development (Docker, Kubernetes, serverless)"
    ],
    "skill_gaps": ["TypeScript", "Docker", "CI/CD pipelines", "GraphQL"],
    "recommended_certifications": ["AWS Developer Associate", "Meta React Developer"],
    "resume_tips": [
      "Add quantifiable impact to your project descriptions (e.g., 'reduced load time by 40%')",
      "Include a GitHub link prominently in your contact section"
    ]
  }
}
```

You can also generate a role-specific learning roadmap using `POST /guidance/role-roadmap` by specifying your target role, current skills, and experience level.

## AI Chat

The AI Chat assistant answers career questions in the context of your resume. Send a POST request to `/ai/chat` with your question and your resume data, and the assistant responds with personalized advice.

```bash
curl -X POST http://localhost:8000/ai/chat \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "query": "I want to transition into data science. What steps should I take given my current skills?",
    "resume_data": {
      "skills": {
        "technical": ["Python", "React", "MongoDB"],
        "area_of_interest": ["Full Stack Development"]
      },
      "education": {
        "bachelor": { "degree": "B.E. Computer Science", "cgpa": "8.45" }
      }
    }
  }'
```

<ParamField body="query" type="string" required>
  Your career question. Be as specific as possible — the more context you provide, the more targeted the advice.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="resume_data" type="object">
  Your structured resume data. Pass the `structured_info` object from the upload response. The AI uses this as context to personalize its answer.
</ParamField>

**Example response:**

```json
{
  "response": "Given your Python background and experience with MongoDB, you already have two of the most critical skills for a data science role. I'd recommend the following path: 1) Start with NumPy, Pandas, and Matplotlib to build your data manipulation and visualization skills. 2) Work through a structured machine learning course (fast.ai or Andrew Ng's ML Specialization on Coursera). 3) Build 2–3 end-to-end projects — for example, a churn prediction model or a recommendation engine — and publish them on GitHub. 4) Learn SQL deeply, as most data science interviews include SQL rounds. 5) Once comfortable, explore scikit-learn and then move to deep learning with TensorFlow or PyTorch."
}
```

<Tip>
  Keep your resume updated every time you gain a new skill or complete a project. Resume Insight re-runs the full analysis on each upload, so fresher data means more accurate role predictions and better-targeted career advice.
</Tip>
