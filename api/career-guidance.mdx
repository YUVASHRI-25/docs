Resume Insight provides two endpoints under `/guidance` to help you move from knowing your resume's weaknesses to having a concrete plan to fix them. The first endpoint — `POST /guidance/generate` — takes your parsed resume data and returns a comprehensive career guidance report: skill gaps, learning paths, project ideas, certificate recommendations, and a weekly improvement schedule, all tailored to your actual profile. The second endpoint — `POST /guidance/role-roadmap` — takes a target job role and your current skills, then generates a phase-by-phase learning roadmap with milestones, projects, and a realistic timeline to get you job-ready.

---

## POST /guidance/generate

Generate a full AI-powered career guidance report from your resume data. This endpoint analyzes your technical skills, projects, certifications, and areas of interest, then produces a structured plan covering everything from skill gaps to a week-by-week improvement schedule.

**Auth:** Not required

**Content-Type:** `application/json`

### Request Parameters

<ParamField body="resume_data" type="object" required>
  Structured resume data in the same format returned by
  `POST /resume/upload_resume` or `POST /user/upload_resume`. Pass the `data`
  field from those responses directly as the value of `resume_data`. The object
  must contain at least a `skills` key for meaningful guidance to be generated.
</ParamField>

### Example Request

```bash
curl -X POST http://localhost:8000/guidance/generate \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "resume_data": {
      "name": "Sneha Reddy",
      "email": "sneha.reddy@email.com",
      "skills": {
        "technical": ["Python", "Pandas", "SQL", "Matplotlib"],
        "soft": ["Communication", "Teamwork"],
        "area_of_interest": ["Data Science", "Machine Learning"]
      },
      "education": {
        "degree": "B.Sc Statistics",
        "institution": "Osmania University",
        "year": "2024"
      },
      "projects": [
        {
          "title": "Sales Forecasting Model",
          "description": "Built a linear regression model to predict monthly sales for a retail dataset."
        }
      ],
      "certifications": ["Google Data Analytics – Coursera"]
    }
  }'
```

### Response Fields

<ResponseField name="guidance" type="object">
  The top-level wrapper object containing all guidance sections.

  <Expandable title="Expand guidance fields">
    <ResponseField name="guidance.technical_skills" type="array">
      Your detected technical skills, each with a `name` and estimated `level`
      (`beginner`, `intermediate`, or `advanced`) inferred from your projects
      and experience.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="guidance.missing_skills" type="array">
      Skills that are important for your detected career track but absent from
      your resume. Each item has a `name` and a `reason` explaining why the
      skill matters for your profile.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="guidance.learning_paths" type="array">
      One or more recommended learning tracks (e.g., Data Science, Web
      Development, Machine Learning / AI). Each path lists a `track` name,
      `topics`, `tools`, `exercises`, and `projects`, along with an
      `estimated_time_weeks` value.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="guidance.project_ideas" type="array">
      Project ideas tailored to your interests and skill level. Each idea
      includes a `title`, `type` (`mini`, `portfolio`, or `capstone`), and a
      `description` of what to build.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="guidance.certificate_recommendations" type="array">
      Certificates worth pursuing — and some to avoid. Each item has a `name`,
      `value_level` (`high`, `medium`, or `low`), a `recommendation`
      (`pursue` or `avoid`), and a `reason`.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="guidance.role_matching" type="array">
      Roles your profile currently matches, with a `match_percentage`, lists of
      `matched_skills` and `missing_skills`, and additional skills recommended
      to close the gap.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="guidance.weak_skills" type="array">
      Generic or low-value skills detected on your resume that add little
      signal for hiring managers. Each item includes the skill `name` and a
      `reason` for removing or de-emphasizing it.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="guidance.recommended_tech_stacks" type="array">
      One or more tech stack recommendations suited to your career direction.
      Each item has a `stack` name and a `reason` explaining why it fits your
      profile.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="guidance.career_clarity_summary" type="object">
      A short summary object containing `primary_alignment` (your most likely
      career track), `aligned_roles` (best-fit job titles), `roles_to_avoid`,
      and `reasoning`.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="guidance.weekly_schedule" type="array">
      A 4–8 week improvement schedule. Each week entry includes a `week`
      number, `focus` statement, `topics` to cover, `practice_tasks`, and
      `checkpoints` to assess your progress.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## POST /guidance/role-roadmap

Generate a personalized, phase-by-phase learning roadmap for a specific job role. This endpoint compares your current skills against the requirements for the role you want, calculates a readiness score, and produces a structured plan with learning phases, project ideas, and a realistic timeline.

**Auth:** Not required

**Content-Type:** `application/json`

### Request Parameters

<ParamField body="target_role" type="string" required>
  The job role you want to prepare for. Use a clear, specific title such as
  `"Frontend Developer"`, `"Data Scientist"`, or `"Cybersecurity Analyst"`.
  The AI uses this to determine the required skill set and tailor the roadmap.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="current_skills" type="string[]" required>
  An array of your current technical skills as plain strings. For best results,
  pass the `data.skills.technical` array returned by the resume upload endpoint
  directly. An empty array is accepted and produces a full beginner roadmap.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="experience_level" type="string" default="fresher">
  Your current experience level. Accepted values: `fresher`, `junior`, `mid`.
  Defaults to `"fresher"` if omitted. This adjusts the depth of the roadmap
  and the difficulty of recommended projects.
</ParamField>

### Example Request

```bash
curl -X POST http://localhost:8000/guidance/role-roadmap \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "target_role": "Full Stack Developer",
    "current_skills": ["Python", "HTML", "CSS", "Git"],
    "experience_level": "fresher"
  }'
```

### Response Fields

<ResponseField name="roadmap" type="object">
  The complete role-specific learning roadmap.

  <Expandable title="Expand roadmap fields">
    <ResponseField name="roadmap.target_role" type="string">
      The role this roadmap was generated for, echoed back from the request.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="roadmap.match_percentage" type="number">
      How ready you currently are for the role, as a percentage from 0 to 100.
      Calculated by comparing your `current_skills` against the role's core
      requirements.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="roadmap.skills_you_have" type="array">
      Skills from your `current_skills` list that are relevant to the target
      role. Each item has `name`, `status` (`"have"`), and `relevance`
      (`high`, `medium`, or `low`).
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="roadmap.skills_to_learn" type="array">
      Skills you need to acquire to become job-ready for the target role. Each
      item has `name`, `priority` (`high`, `medium`, or `low`), `reason`
      explaining why it is needed, and `learning_time` (e.g., `"2 weeks"`).
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="roadmap.roadmap_phases" type="array">
      Ordered learning phases, each building on the last. Every phase includes
      `phase` (number), `title`, `duration` (e.g., `"4-6 weeks"`), `focus`,
      `skills` (array), `action_items` (array), `milestone`, and `resources`
      (array of recommended learning resources).
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="roadmap.projects" type="array">
      Project ideas to build alongside the roadmap. Each project has `title`,
      `description`, `difficulty` (`beginner`, `intermediate`, or `advanced`),
      `skills_used`, and `duration`.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="roadmap.estimated_timeline" type="string">
      Total estimated time to complete the roadmap (e.g., `"12-16 weeks"`),
      based on the number of phases and your current readiness.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="roadmap.tips" type="array">
      Actionable tips for staying on track, building a strong portfolio, and
      preparing for interviews in the target role.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

---

<Tip>
  Use the `data.skills.technical` array from your resume upload response
  directly as the `current_skills` value. This ensures the roadmap is generated
  from the exact skills Resume Insight detected on your resume, giving you the
  most accurate gap analysis.
</Tip>
