Resume Insight reads your uploaded resume and converts it into structured, machine-readable data in seconds. Whether you upload a PDF or a Word document, the AI-powered parser pulls out every key detail — your name and contact information, educational history, technical and soft skills, internships, projects, and certifications — so the rest of the platform can score, analyze, and guide you accurately. You never need to re-enter your information manually.

## Supported Formats

Resume Insight accepts the following file types:

| Format | Extension | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| PDF | `.pdf` | Best compatibility; recommended for most users |
| Word Document | `.docx` | Fully supported |
| Legacy Word | `.doc` | Supported; convert to `.docx` for best results |

For optimal parsing accuracy, keep your resume under **5 MB**. Larger files are accepted but may take slightly longer to process.

<Note>
  You will get the most accurate extraction results when your resume uses clean, standard formatting — avoid multi-column layouts, text boxes, headers/footers with important content, or embedded images containing text. Plain single-column PDFs parse best.
</Note>

## Uploading a Resume

To parse a resume without saving it to an account, send a multipart form request to the raw upload endpoint. No authentication is required for this endpoint.

**Endpoint:** `POST /resume/upload_resume`

```bash
curl -X POST http://localhost:8000/resume/upload_resume \
  -F "file=@/path/to/your_resume.pdf"
```

## Resume Data in User Context

When you upload a resume while logged in to your account, use the authenticated endpoint instead. This saves the extracted data — along with your ATS score, detected role, and AI skill suggestions — so you can access it later from your dashboard or via the history endpoint.

**Endpoint:** `POST /user/upload_resume`

```bash
curl -X POST http://localhost:8000/user/upload_resume \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <your_token>" \
  -F "email=priya.ramesh@email.com" \
  -F "file=@/path/to/your_resume.pdf"
```

**Retrieve saved resume data:**

```bash
curl http://localhost:8000/user/history/priya.ramesh@email.com \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <your_token>"
```

The history response includes your full `structured_info`, `ats_score`, `ats_breakdown`, `detected_role`, `suggested_skills`, and the timestamp of your last upload.

<Note>
  The raw `POST /resume/upload_resume` endpoint processes and returns your resume data but **does not save it to your account**. To persist your results and access them later from your dashboard or the history endpoint, always use `POST /user/upload_resume` with your email and a valid auth token.
</Note>
